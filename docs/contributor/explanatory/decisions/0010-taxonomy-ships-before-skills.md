## Context

The proof of concept shipped six draft skills alongside the taxonomy — procedures for triage, decision recording, reconciliation, planning, consumer docs, and adoption.
Rebuilding the project raised the question of whether skills belong in the first release.

## Options considered

- **Ship the draft skills with the rebuild.**
  Ruled out: the drafts predate the frame renames and the gap-meaning grounding, so they teach superseded terms, and rewriting them now would freeze procedure around a model that had only just settled.
  Procedures harden conventions; conventions should harden after the model has survived contact with use.
- **Commit now to a specific MVP skill set as a stated goal.**
  Ruled out: which verbs deserve procedures first is still an open question, and recording an undecided question as a settled goal misfiles it — undecided input belongs in the inbox until deciding makes it splittable.

## Decision

The first release is the taxonomy and its carriers: the contracts, the conventions, and the documentation — no skills.
The docs acknowledge skills as the third carrier and constrain their future shape (they will speak to the item interface, not storage details).
Which skills to build, and in what order, sits in the inbox with a pointer to the draft procedures as raw material.
